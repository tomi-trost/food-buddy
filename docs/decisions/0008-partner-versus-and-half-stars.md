# 0008 — Partner "Versus" analytics and half-star taste
Date: 2026-10-04 · Status: accepted

**Decision (analytics):** Insights has Overview and **Versus** tabs. Versus is a head-to-head scoreboard over the week (fiber-goal days, avg fiber, avg protein, calorie accuracy, meals rated) plus a selectable weekly challenge (Fiber race / Protein push / On target) with progress bars for both.
**Decision (rating, revises 0003):** Taste supports half stars (0.5 steps) — tap the left/right half of a star. Make-again and effort axes unchanged.
**Consequences:** Needs per-user daily logs in the real data model. Keep it friendly — no public shaming; consider opt-in goals per person.

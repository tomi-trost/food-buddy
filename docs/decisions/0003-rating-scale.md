# 0003 — Three-axis rating per person
Date: 2026-10-04 · Status: accepted (revisit after trying it)

**Context:** Ratings drive which recipes get promoted into the weekly menu.
**Decision:** Each person rates Taste (1–5 stars), Make again? (yes/maybe/no), Worth the effort? (yes/meh/no). Combined score = mean of the three (mapped to 1–5), averaged across raters.
**Alternatives:** Single 1–5 stars; thumbs + 10-point.
**Consequences:** Richer signal for menu generation (effort matters when time is limited); slightly more taps — keep the sheet fast.

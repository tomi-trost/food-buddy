# 0011 — Sugar tracking and a separate Sweets log
Date: 2026-10-04 · Status: accepted

**Decision:** Sugar is tracked as a nutrient on meals and in daily totals (goal 50 g, configurable). Sweets (chocolate, cookies, soda, …) are logged separately from meals via a quick sheet on Home; they add to daily kcal/sugar but have their own Insights tab (7-day sugar bars with goal line, treat-free days, sweets count, partner comparison).
**Consequences:** Needs a sweets catalogue and optional custom entry in the real app.
